# AI-Assisted Content and Brand Voice System

## Project Name
AI-Assisted Content and Brand Voice System for Quanthos

## Project Idea
This project develops a repeatable AI-assisted content system for Quanthos, a data, business intelligence, and AI consultancy serving organizations in Egypt and the GCC.

The system defines the brand voice, maps content pillars to funnel stages and customer objections, plans a 30-day campaign, and demonstrates how one idea can be adapted into multiple content formats. It also includes reusable AI prompts and an n8n workflow for generating drafts that require human review before publication.

## Work Plan
1. Define Quanthos positioning and target audience.
2. Document the brand voice and content pillars.
3. Build a 30-day content calendar with platforms, objectives, formats, and KPIs.
4. Create a hook library using different copywriting frameworks.
5. Repurpose one core idea into a post, carousel, video, and ad copy.
6. Prepare prompts for ideation, writing, rewriting, and quality review.
7. Build and review the n8n content-drafting workflow.
8. Review the final materials for completeness and consistency.

## Roles and Responsibilities
- **Osama Naguib:** Project planning, brand strategy, audience and funnel mapping, content calendar, copywriting examples, prompt design, n8n workflow preparation, and final review.
- **Human reviewer:** Check factual accuracy, brand voice, evidence, and approval of AI-generated drafts before publication.

## KPIs
Campaign targets are planning benchmarks and should be adjusted after reviewing actual account performance.

- Awareness: relevant audience reach and video views.
- Consideration: saves, shares, substantive comments, and qualified profile visits.
- Conversion: qualified inquiries and booked diagnostic calls.
- Workflow quality: 30 draft content items generated and reviewed before any are scheduled or published.
